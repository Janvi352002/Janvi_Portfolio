01 / About Me

I never planned to become
an AI Engineer.

I never planned to become an AI Engineer.

Growing up, I imagined myself doing something completely different — perhaps becoming a public speaker, a lawyer, a politician, a social worker, or simply doing something where I could work with people, solve problems, and create an impact.

But technology had a different plan for me.

I still remember the little Nokia phone my father had. I would play games on it, fascinated by something that seemed so simple. Then came camera phones, physical keyboards, touchscreens, smartphones, and an entire generation of technology evolving before my eyes.

From 2G to 3G, 4G and now 5G.
From simple mobile games to devices that have become an integral part of our lives.

That evolution made me curious.
I started asking myself: How does all of this actually work? And what comes next?

Eventually, I realized I didn't just want to use technology — I wanted to understand it, build with it, and be part of what comes next.

And then came AI.

The rise of Machine Learning, Generative AI, automation and now Agentic AI completely changed the way I looked at technology. Ideas that once felt like science fiction were becoming real products, businesses and solutions.

Today, I'm building my journey in AI Engineering, with a strong interest in Python, Machine Learning, Generative AI, AI Agents, automation, data, cloud and intelligent systems.

But technology is only one part of who I am.

I'm equally fascinated by business, startups and the people behind them.

Whether it's B2B, telecom, logistics, SaaS, AI, or an entirely different industry — I enjoy understanding how a business works, what problem it solves, who its customers are, and how technology can make it better.

I enjoy interacting with clients, understanding their challenges, translating business problems into technical solutions, and being part of conversations beyond just writing code.

I don't see myself as someone who belongs to only one box.

I'm a tech-driven, business-oriented problem solver who enjoys connecting technology, people and ideas.

I enjoy learning across domains, taking ownership, exploring opportunities, and stepping into leadership when there's a problem that needs to be solved.

I'm also fortunate to have built a 10k+ LinkedIn community, where I learn, share ideas, exchange perspectives and connect with people across industries.

I believe the future belongs to people who can understand both sides of the equation:

Technology that works.
Business that matters.
People who make it happen.

Still curious. Still learning. Still building. 🚀
